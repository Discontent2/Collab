## Quick visual reference

The requested look is a **1975–1980-style hand-inked American prehistoric monster-adventure comic**, with pulp fantasy ruins and Ray Harryhausen-inspired miniature-monster physicality. Keep massive, expressive dinosaur silhouettes; thick black contours; crosshatching and stippling; aged four-color halftone printing; yellow parchment captions; deep crimson monsters contrasted with slate-blue ruins.

For full specifications and reusable prompts, always consult the [Art Style Guide](art-style-guide.md). This index is a navigational summary and does not supersede the guide.
