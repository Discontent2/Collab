## Production Notes and Character Locks

- **D-REX:** Enormous crimson tyrannosaur, dark markings, scarred hide, yellow-orange eyes, powerful skull with bony ridges.
- **ROOK:** Small olive-green raptor with dark markings and restrained red accents. The audience's primary observational viewpoint.
- **SLATE:** Large blue-gray predatory dinosaur, comparable in scale to D-Rex but different skull and color.
- **ASHCREST:** Pale beige carnivore with rust-red head and neck crest.
- **OLD STEGOSAUR:** Broad, orderly dorsal plates and herbivorous head; a protective shield for smaller animals.
- **GRAVENMAW:** Charcoal-black armored, low-slung quadrupedal carnivore, wedge-shaped reinforced skull, interlocking crooked teeth, irregular osteoderms. The first adult bears a pale scar above its **left eye**.
- **GRAVENMAW MATRIARCH:** Enormous weathered adult female, shown on the final splash only. Her head dramatically dwarfs the original fortress gateway.

All animals must act through stance, eye-line, breathing, recoil, approach, hesitation, warning posture and roar, never spoken dialogue or human expressions. Review the [Art Style Guide](art-style-guide.md), [Gravenmaw Bestiary](creature-gravenmaw-ancient-apex-predator.md), and [Characters & Continuity](characters-and-continuity.md).

### Fixed Courtyard Geography

- **North:** Towering fortress and sealed monumental gate.
- **East:** Broken drainage arch and passages near the fortress, the first Gravenmaw's entrance.
- **West:** Broken staircase, Slate's entrance.
- **South:** Fallen columns, elevated ruined ledges and Rook's sheltered vantage; smaller dinosaurs' refuge.
- **Approach:** D-Rex enters from the eastern ridge, descends via ruined avenue, and approaches the courtyard beneath the eastern arch.

**Lighting progression:** amber late afternoon → orange sunset → long blue-gray shadows → near-black subterranean chamber with residual warm light above.

# PAGE 1 — THE KING RETURNS

**Layout:** Four panels: one enormous horizontal establishing panel, three smaller panels.  
**Purpose:** Establish the kingdom's scale, ancient architecture and D-Rex's dominance.

### Panel 1 — Wide establishing shot
**ART:** Spectacular panoramic view of enormous ruined prehistoric city surrounded by jungle. Broken stone towers rise above vegetation; the monumental fortress dominates the northern skyline. Vines and giant roots split old masonry. Late-afternoon sun behind mountains. On a high rocky ridge in the right foreground stands the massive crimson D-Rex, silhouetted against sky. He and the city should look overwhelmingly large and impossibly old.

**TITLE LETTERING:** D-REX / THE KING OF THE RUINS  
**CAPTION:** "In a forgotten kingdom, the stones remember what the living have forgotten."

### Panel 2 — Close-up
**ART:** Extreme close-up of D-Rex's yellow-orange eye among scarlet scales and old dark scars. In the reflection, tiny distant animals move between ruin buildings. **No caption.**

### Panel 3 — Low angle
**ART:** Immense clawed foot lands heavily on ridge; stones cascade and dust rises around toes. Weight and ground compression must be unmistakable.  
**SFX:** THUDD

### Panel 4 — Wide dramatic shot
**ART:** D-Rex throws his enormous head back and roars, jaws completely open. Birds burst from the treetops around the ruins.  
**SFX:** RRRRRAAAAAHHH!  
**CAPTION:** "The red tyrant had returned."

**PAGE TURN:** How the smaller dinosaurs respond.

# Editorial and Illustration Review Notes

## Acting, Motivations and Storytelling

- No spoken dinosaur dialogue. Threat, warning, aggression, protection and alarm live in eye-lines, posture and actions.
- **D-Rex:** territorial tyrant whose possibly protective choices are revealed indirectly; never make him merely kind or human.
- **Rook:** small observational focal point: first senses ground vibrations, then gate movements, then hidden population.
- **Slate:** rival who mistakes threat for test of strength and learns his teeth cannot pierce thick Gravenmaw armor.
- **Ashcrest:** brave, reckless attacker who reveals dangers of the armored animal.
- **Old stegosaur:** defensive refuge for young animals; not a verbal or tactical leader.
- **Gravenmaw:** an ancient natural predator and displaced ecological population, not a villain or constructed weapon.
- **Matriarch:** huge adult female with natural survival motivation, not an evil queen.

## Visual and Geographic Continuity

- D-Rex is red with marked hide, yellow-orange eye, distinctive massive ridged skull.
- Slate must be visibly different from D-Rex and retain blue-gray hide.
- Ashcrest remains pale with rust-red crest.
- Stegosaur has orderly dorsal plates and herbivore head; never give it Gravenmaw's predatory dentition.
- Adult Gravenmaw has irregular armor and characteristic pale **left-eye** scar.
- Matriarch's scale uses original fortress gateway, fallen walls and tiny foreground dinosaurs as reference.
- West = Slate stairs; east = drainage arch / D-Rex approach; north = sealed fortress; south = Rook ledges and smaller animals.
- Sunset shifts gradually toward nocturnal blue shadow; cold dark underworld only appears at rupture.

## Art and Lettering

Follow [the saved Art Style Guide](art-style-guide.md): hand-drawn 1975–1980 Bronze Age monster-adventure paneling, thick contours, feathered black crosshatching, stippling, misregistered four-color halftone, parchment captions, heavy black gutters and distressed bold type. Keep the tactile miniature effect. **Do not imitate glossy digital photorealism.**

Differentiate roars: D-Rex territory, Slate defiance, Ashcrest contact, Gravenmaw low subsonic warning, matriarch colossal final call. Silence before the armored animal strikes is more frightening than constant noise.
